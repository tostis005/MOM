Rules:
- Create articles strictly in this numeric order.
- Do not infer, replace, reorder, skip, or invent topics.
- Spanish and English versions share the same `article_number` and `translation_group`; English must be independently localized while preserving the approved search intent.
- Before proposing future topics, review all topic files in `content/topics/` to reduce duplication and search-intent cannibalization.
